一句回覆、一則帖文，會唔會令事情愈搞愈大？

招聘廣告、宣傳優惠、客戶投訴、員工回應，都可能引起外界關注。事情未發生時，可以先檢視容易出現誤解的地方；事情發生後，就要核實事實、安排溝通及跟進改善。

公關危機應對避坑

你可能正遇到這些問題

01

招聘、優惠或宣傳帖文，出街前有甚麼要留意？

常見情境

團隊覺得只是普通帖文，發布後卻被質疑條件不清、說法不當，甚至與實際安排不符。

先釐清

事實和數字是否準確、重要條件是否清楚，以及不同讀者可能如何理解。涉及法定要求的內容亦需另行核實。

宇見可以協助

從資訊完整度、用字及受眾理解檢視文案，找出容易引起爭議的部分，整理發布前的確認事項。

02

客戶公開投訴，應該即刻回應嗎？

常見情境

帖文開始被轉發，有同事主張立即反駁，亦有人認為不要回應。

先釐清

哪些事實已確認、是否仍有損害持續發生，以及客戶和其他受影響者最需要知道甚麼。

宇見可以協助

整理事件時間線，規劃初步回應、直接聯絡及後續更新，避免倉促作出無法兌現的承諾。

03

對方只講一半，可以公開全部對話澄清嗎？

常見情境

機構希望貼出截圖自證清白，但對話包含客戶、員工或第三方資料。

先釐清

真正需要澄清哪個事實、哪些內容與問題相關，以及有沒有私隱或保密事項。

宇見可以協助

整理澄清重點，比較回應方式；涉及資料披露或法律風險，先交相關專業人士審視。

04

記者突然來電，前線應該點答？

常見情境

記者要求限時回覆，接聽同事未掌握背景，也不知道誰有權發言。

先釐清

查詢內容、回覆時間，以及內部由誰核實、審批和發出回應。

宇見可以協助

整理查詢接收及轉交程序，準備回應重點和可能被追問的問題。

05

老闆、前線、社交平台各有各講，點收拾？

常見情境

客服已作承諾，主管卻表示仍在調查，社交平台又出現另一種說法。

先釐清

已說過甚麼、答應過甚麼，以及目前哪些資料可以確認。

宇見可以協助

整理共用事實、常見問答和授權範圍，建立更新方式，減少回覆互相矛盾。

06

事情未查清，應唔應該道歉？

常見情境

怕不道歉顯得冷漠，又怕把未確認的事情全部承認。

先釐清

已知影響、待查事實和可以立即採取的改善措施。

宇見可以協助

分清關懷、事實說明、處理行動及待查部分，協助準備回應；涉及責任承認交由律師審視。

07

退款、補償定重做，點避免愈傾愈亂？

常見情境

前線為平息事件不斷加碼，客戶仍不滿意，內部亦不清楚承諾是否可執行。

先釐清

實際訴求、已確認的影響、過往承諾及處理權限。

宇見可以協助

整理方案和執行條件，協助說明處理範圍、時間與完成確認。

08

員工或合作方出事，機構應該點交代？

常見情境

事件由員工、供應商或承辦商引起，但客戶和公眾直接要求機構回應。

先釐清

機構已掌握多少資料、服務是否受影響，以及可以立即採取甚麼安排。

宇見可以協助

整理各方紀錄、受影響範圍和溝通次序，準備可核實的說明及跟進安排。

09

網上每個留言都要回？刪除又會點？

常見情境

留言愈來愈多，團隊忙於逐則爭辯，或急於刪除，卻未有一致處理原則。

先釐清

哪些是具體投訴、疑問、重複訊息或其他內容，以及平台規則和機構既有管理安排。

宇見可以協助

分類整理留言和回應需要，準備核心問答及升級條件，讓處理方式有一致依據。

10

風波平息之後，點避免再發生？

常見情境

網上熱度下降，但原有服務問題、授權不清或跟進遺漏仍然存在。

先釐清

事件如何開始、甚麼令問題擴大，以及承諾的改善是否完成。

宇見可以協助

整理事件檢討，將發現轉成發布前檢查、投訴記錄、授權和跟進流程。

Public relations crisis response and pitfalls

You may be facing these situations

01

What should we check before publishing a recruitment or promotional post?

Common situation

A routine post is criticised for unclear conditions, inappropriate wording or inconsistencies with actual arrangements.

Clarify first

Whether facts and figures are accurate, important conditions are clear and different readers may interpret them differently. Statutory requirements also need separate verification.

How U Vision can help

Review completeness, wording and audience understanding, identify potential points of dispute and prepare a pre-publication checklist.

02

A customer complains publicly. Should we respond immediately?

Common situation

A post is being shared. Some colleagues want to rebut it immediately; others advise silence.

Clarify first

Which facts are confirmed, whether harm is continuing, and what customers and others affected most need to know.

How U Vision can help

Build the timeline and plan initial responses, direct contact and subsequent updates, avoiding commitments that cannot be delivered.

03

Can we publish the full conversation to correct a partial account?

Common situation

The organisation wants to publish screenshots, but they contain customer, staff or third-party information.

Clarify first

Which facts really need clarification, what is relevant, and whether privacy or confidentiality is involved.

How U Vision can help

Organise clarification points and compare response options. Disclosure or legal risks should first be reviewed by the appropriate professionals.

04

A journalist calls unexpectedly. How should frontline staff respond?

Common situation

A journalist wants a reply by a deadline, but the recipient lacks background and does not know who may speak.

Clarify first

The questions, deadline and internal responsibility for checking, approving and issuing a response.

How U Vision can help

Organise enquiry intake and referral procedures, prepare response points and anticipate follow-up questions.

05

Management, frontline staff and social channels give conflicting accounts. What now?

Common situation

Customer service has made a promise, a manager says investigations are continuing, and social media gives another account.

Clarify first

What has been said and promised, and which information can currently be confirmed.

How U Vision can help

Organise shared facts, FAQs and authority limits, and establish an update process to reduce contradictions.

06

Should we apologise before all the facts are known?

Common situation

You worry that silence appears uncaring, but do not want to accept unconfirmed allegations.

Clarify first

Known impacts, facts still being checked and improvements that can be made immediately.

How U Vision can help

Separate concern, factual explanation, action and open questions when preparing a response. Admissions of liability should be reviewed by a lawyer.

07

Refund, compensation or rework: how do we keep discussions clear?

Common situation

Frontline staff keep increasing offers to settle matters, but customers remain dissatisfied and delivery is uncertain.

Clarify first

The actual request, confirmed impact, previous commitments and staff authority.

How U Vision can help

Organise options and delivery conditions, and help explain scope, timing and completion checks.

08

A staff member or partner causes an incident. What should the organisation say?

Common situation

An employee, supplier or contractor causes an incident, but customers and the public expect the organisation to respond.

Clarify first

What the organisation knows, whether service is affected and which immediate measures are possible.

How U Vision can help

Organise records, affected areas and the communication sequence, preparing verifiable explanations and follow-up arrangements.

09

Should every online comment get a reply? What about deletion?

Common situation

Comments multiply. Staff argue with each one or delete them hastily, without a consistent approach.

Clarify first

Which posts are specific complaints, questions, repeated messages or other content, and what platform rules and existing moderation arrangements apply.

How U Vision can help

Classify comments and response needs, prepare core answers and escalation criteria, and establish a consistent basis for handling them.

10

How do we prevent a repeat after the controversy fades?

Common situation

Online attention falls, but the service issue, unclear authority or missed follow-up remains.

Clarify first

How the incident began, what made it worse and whether promised improvements were completed.

How U Vision can help

Review the incident and turn findings into publication checks, complaint records, authorisation and follow-up procedures.

宇見如何協助 · 經營規則與項目可行性分析

有構想，先睇清在澳門行唔行得通。

想開展新業務，但唔清楚政策容唔容許、要辦甚麼手續；見到別人做到，又唔肯定自己的方案是否適用。

看看常見情境 ↓查詢我們能否協助
宇見可以怎樣幫

宇見從本地政策、申請要求及營運安排入手，協助你找出影響落地的關鍵條件，整理需要確認的問題，再比較不同方案的成本、限制及下一步。

政策支持、申請資格同實際營運，需要逐項看清。

一個構想能否落實，往往取決於具體安排：誰提出申請、在哪裏提供服務、由誰營運和收費，以及需要經過哪些程序。

把這些條件提早整理清楚，有助你在簽約、購置設備或投入更多資源前，作出更有依據的決定。

經營規則與項目可行性分析

你可能正遇到這些問題

01

外地或同行做得到，我是否可以照做？

常見情境

見到外地有成熟模式，或澳門已有類似服務，便想按同一方式經營，卻不清楚對方是否有不同的場地、批准或營運安排。

先釐清

實際提供甚麼服務、向誰收費、由誰營運，以及自己的方案與參考例子有哪些差異。表面相似，未必代表適用條件相同。

宇見可以怎樣幫

拆解服務和營運流程，對照本地公開政策與行政要求，找出不能直接照搬的環節，整理待確認事項及可調整方向。

02

有場地、有設備，是否就可以開展業務？

常見情境

已找到地方，設備亦有報價，但場地用途、設施條件和申請程序是否配合？擔心簽約付款後，才發現項目落實不到。

先釐清

業務內容與場地、設備是否配合，哪些條件需要事先確認，以及哪些投入取決於後續批准。

宇見可以怎樣幫

把方案與公開申請要求逐項對照，整理投入前需要核實的條件、所需資料及辦理次序，協助你看清是否需要調整場地或營運安排。

03

合作模式、收費方式傾好，是否符合本地要求？

常見情境

一方提供場地、一方持有設備，另一方營運及收費；亦可能採用會員制、套餐或按次收費。商業安排談妥了，卻未確認是否配合申請和營運要求。

先釐清

誰申請、誰提供服務、誰收款，各方實際承擔甚麼工作。服務名稱和收費方式，也需要連同實際運作一併說明。

宇見可以怎樣幫

梳理各方角色、設備權屬和收費流程，整理不同方案的差異，協助向主管部門提出具體查詢，找出需要調整或進一步尋求專業意見的部分。

04

政策鼓勵這個行業，自己的項目是否適用？

常見情境

看到政策支持某個產業，想申請資助或開展項目，但不清楚自己的服務是否屬於支持範圍，也未分清資助條件與營運要求。

先釐清

政策支持甚麼、誰可申請、哪些項目或開支適用，以及實際營運還有哪些條件需要確認。

宇見可以怎樣幫

分別整理政策支持、申請資格及營運要求，對照項目的具體內容，列明資料缺口、待確認條件和落地步驟。

05

想申請人才計劃，自己的條件是否相符？

常見情境

有專業資格和多年工作經驗，希望來澳發展，但不清楚自己的背景、專業成果及來澳發展安排，是否對應相關計劃。

先釐清

適用計劃、申請時間及公開要求，以及現有資料能否清楚證明相關經歷和成果。

宇見可以怎樣幫

按相關計劃的公開條件，梳理個人背景及證明資料，初步對照要求，找出資料缺口和需要向主管部門確認的事項。正式資格及審批結果由主管部門決定。

06

項目涉及幾個部門，應該先辦哪一步？

常見情境

場地、設施、業務和人員各有要求，文件互相牽連；問過不同部門，仍不清楚先後次序。

先釐清

各部門處理哪一部分、哪些文件有前置條件，以及哪項尚未確認的安排會影響整個方案。

宇見可以怎樣幫

整理程序、文件及查詢清單，標示前置條件和跟進次序，協助準備有具體背景的部門查詢，減少重複準備與往返。

07

原本方案遇到限制，還有沒有調整空間？

常見情境

初步查詢後發現部分安排難以落實，但已投入時間和資源，不知道應修改方案、分階段推進，還是暫時停止。

先釐清

受限制的是核心服務，還是場地、設備、合作角色或營運方式；哪些條件已確認，哪些仍有待查明。

宇見可以怎樣幫

整理可調整的方向，比較各方案的成本、限制及需要再次確認的事項，協助你決定下一階段值得投入甚麼。

資料查咗好多，宇見如何幫你整理成下一步？

按個案需要及約定範圍，我們可協助整理：

落地條件清單

哪些條件已有資料支持，哪些仍待確認。

資料缺口及查詢重點

欠甚麼文件，需要向哪個部門或專業人士問甚麼。

方案比較

不同安排涉及的成本、限制、前置條件和不確定性。

行動次序

下一步由誰、用甚麼資料、先處理哪件事。

讓你在推進、調整或暫緩之前，清楚知道決定建基於甚麼，還有哪些未知需要處理。

下一步

先把構想講清楚，再看可行的下一步。

聯絡時，可先簡單說明：

  1. 想在澳門開展甚麼業務，或申請甚麼計劃？
  2. 目前準備到哪一步，已查過甚麼資料？
  3. 最想釐清哪個問題，是否有預定時間？

毋須一開始提交身份證或完整商業文件。我們會先了解情況，說明能否協助，再確認服務範圍、費用及資料提交安排。

Business rules and project feasibility

You may be facing these situations

01

Others can do it. Can I use the same model?

Common situation

You see an established model elsewhere, or a similar service in Macao, and want to follow it without knowing whether different premises, approvals or operating arrangements apply.

Clarify first

What service is actually provided, who pays, who operates it, and how your proposal differs from the example. Similar appearances do not necessarily mean the same conditions apply.

How U Vision can help

Map services and operations against published local policies and administrative requirements, identify what cannot be copied directly, and list questions and possible adjustments.

02

I have premises and equipment. Can I start operating?

Common situation

You have found premises and obtained equipment quotations, but are unsure whether their use, facilities and application procedures fit. You worry about discovering barriers after signing and paying.

Clarify first

Whether the business, premises and equipment fit together, which conditions need prior confirmation, and which investments depend on later approval.

How U Vision can help

Compare the proposal with published application requirements, list conditions and documents to verify before investing, and clarify the sequence and any changes needed.

03

Does our partnership and charging model meet local requirements?

Common situation

One party provides premises, another owns equipment and a third operates and collects fees. Memberships, packages or per-use charges may be planned, but application and operating requirements remain unchecked.

Clarify first

Who applies, provides services and collects payments, and what each party actually does. Service names and charging methods need to be explained alongside actual operations.

How U Vision can help

Organise roles, equipment ownership and payment flows, compare options, and prepare specific enquiries to authorities about adjustments or further professional advice.

04

Policy supports this sector. Does my project qualify?

Common situation

You want funding or to launch a project in a supported industry, but are unsure whether the service falls within the scope or how funding conditions differ from operating requirements.

Clarify first

What the policy supports, who may apply, which projects or expenses qualify, and what else must be confirmed for actual operations.

How U Vision can help

Review policy support, eligibility and operating requirements separately against the project, identifying information gaps, outstanding conditions and launch steps.

05

Do I meet the requirements of a talent scheme?

Common situation

You have qualifications and years of experience and hope to develop a career in Macao, but are unsure whether your background, achievements and development plans fit a scheme.

Clarify first

The applicable scheme, application period and published requirements, and whether existing evidence clearly demonstrates your experience and achievements.

How U Vision can help

Organise your background and supporting documents against published conditions, identify gaps and questions for the authority. Formal eligibility and approval are decided by the authority.

06

Several departments are involved. What should I do first?

Common situation

Premises, facilities, operations and staff have different requirements, with interdependent documents. Enquiries to several departments have not clarified the order.

Clarify first

What each department handles, which documents have prerequisites, and which unresolved arrangements affect the whole proposal.

How U Vision can help

Organise procedures, document and enquiry lists, mark prerequisites and follow-up priorities, and prepare enquiries with specific background to reduce repeated preparation and visits.

07

The original proposal faces limits. Can it be adjusted?

Common situation

Initial enquiries reveal difficulties, but time and resources have already been invested. You are unsure whether to revise, proceed in stages or pause.

Clarify first

Whether the restriction affects the core service or the premises, equipment, partnership roles or operating model, and which conditions are confirmed or still unknown.

How U Vision can help

List possible adjustments, compare costs and constraints, and identify points needing further confirmation to help decide what is worth investing in next.
